A SMALL WINDOW INTO THE EXTREME

Nothing escapes.
Except your curiosity.

Every pixel is a photon traced backwards along a Schwarzschild null geodesic. Light can circle the photon sphere, cross the accretion disk repeatedly, or disappear through the event horizon.

The disk and the sky are entirely procedural. Orbital Doppler shifts and gravitational redshift transform the emitted light. This is a non-rotating, idealized black hole; the animated disk is an artistic radiative model, not a GRMHD simulation.
